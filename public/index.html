December 19–20, 2026

A Little Christmas Magic in Los Angeles

Well, this wasn't exactly how we planned on spending the week before Christmas. We were supposed to be in Mexico, enjoying some sunshine, sitting on the beach, and probably having a few drinks without a care in the world. Unfortunately, life had other plans, and that trip is going to have to wait.

But I still wanted to do something special for you, so I put together a little weekend getaway that I hope you'll really enjoy. I know it's not Mexico, but you've got a beautiful hotel, some great food, a little entertainment, and a morning of relaxation and pampering ahead of you. You've got a pretty full schedule, but don't worry, none of it involves actual work. :)

Your Itinerary

Saturday, December 19

Your Christmas Getaway Begins!

  1. Sat, Dec 19 3:00 PM

    Check-In at Conrad Los Angeles

    Conrad Los Angeles

    Welcome to the Conrad! You've got a beautiful hotel in the middle of downtown LA to call home for the next couple of days, so get checked in, settle into your room, and take a little time to relax and enjoy the surroundings. It's not quite an oceanfront suite in Mexico, but I think you'll survive. :)

    Welcome!
  2. Sat, Dec 19 4:00 PM

    Self-Guided Art Tour

    Conrad Los Angeles

    The Conrad has a pretty impressive collection of contemporary artwork throughout the hotel, and they've put together a self-guided tour that allows you to explore everything at your own pace. I thought this would be a neat way to spend an hour, see a little more of the property, and check out some interesting artwork along the way.

  3. Sat, Dec 19 5:00 PM

    Relax & Get Ready for Dinner

    Your room

    Head back to your room, kick your feet up for a bit, and take your time getting ready for the evening. You've got dinner and a show ahead of you, so enjoy a little downtime before heading out for the night.

  4. Sat, Dec 19 5:45 PM

    Dinner at The Beaudry Room or San Laurel

    Conrad Los Angeles

    You've got a couple of options for dinner, depending on what you're in the mood for. My first thought was The Beaudry Room, a really nice cocktail lounge in the hotel with great views, good drinks, and a menu of smaller plates. I thought grabbing a seat at the bar might be a fun, casual way to enjoy dinner, so stop by around 5:45 and see if they have something available.

    If the bar is full, no worries, because you've got a 6:00 reservation at San Laurel, the hotel's signature restaurant from chef José Andrés, which has a Spanish-inspired menu and some pretty spectacular views. Either way, I think you'll have a great dinner!

    6:00 PM reservation at San Laurel
  5. Sat, Dec 19 7:15 PM

    Head to the Ahmanson Theatre

    Ahmanson Theatre

    Once you've finished dinner, it's time to head over to the Ahmanson Theatre for the evening's entertainment. It's just a short distance from the Conrad, so you shouldn't have to worry about fighting your way across LA, and you'll have a little time to get situated before the show begins.

  6. Sat, Dec 19 8:00 PM

    Christmas Carol Goes Wrong

    Ahmanson Theatre

    I wanted to find something fun and Christmas-related for the evening, and this looked pretty entertaining. It's a comedy from the people behind The Play That Goes Wrong, where a theater company attempts to perform the classic A Christmas Carol, but just about everything imaginable goes wrong in the process. It looks like a pretty hilarious take on a traditional Christmas story, so hopefully you'll get some good laughs out of it!

    Showtime!

Sunday, December 20

A Little Relaxation and Pampering

Okay, so there's no beach, no cabana, and nobody bringing you drinks with little umbrellas in them, but I think I've come up with a pretty decent alternative. Today is all about taking it easy, relaxing, and enjoying a little time for yourself without having to worry about much of anything.

  1. Sun, Dec 20 8:00 AM

    Stretch & Restore

    Conrad Los Angeles

    If you're feeling motivated, the Conrad offers a Stretch & Restore session that's designed to improve flexibility, relieve tension, and help you feel a little more refreshed. I thought it might be a nice way to start the morning before your spa treatments, but it's completely optional, so if sleeping in sounds more appealing, I fully support that decision!

    Optional
  2. Sun, Dec 20 9:00 AM

    Breakfast

    Conrad Los Angeles

    Grab some breakfast, enjoy your coffee, and take your time getting the morning started. You've got a couple of spa treatments coming up, so there's really no reason to be in a hurry, and hopefully you'll get to enjoy a nice, relaxing breakfast before heading downstairs for a little pampering.

  3. Sun, Dec 20 10:00 AM

    Quantum Harmonic Therapy

    Conrad Spa

    I came across this while looking through the Conrad's spa offerings, and it sounded pretty interesting. It's a relaxation-focused wellness experience that's intended to help you disconnect, unwind, and enjoy a little peace and quiet. I'm not going to pretend I completely understand all the science behind it, but it sounded like something a little different that might be fun to try, so you'll have to let me know what you think!

    Spa treatment
  4. Sun, Dec 20 11:00 AM

    Intuitive Massage (60 Minutes)

    Conrad Spa

    Now this one I'm pretty confident you'll enjoy! You've got a full hour of massage therapy that's personalized to what your body needs, whether that's relieving some tension or simply enjoying a little uninterrupted relaxation. I figured this would be a pretty good way to finish your spa morning, and hopefully you'll walk out feeling completely relaxed and refreshed.

    Spa treatment
  5. Sun, Dec 20 2:00 PM

    Check-Out

    Conrad Los Angeles

    Unfortunately, all good things have to come to an end, so you'll have until 2:00 to enjoy the hotel, gather your things, and get checked out. Hopefully you'll be heading home feeling relaxed, refreshed, and maybe just a little spoiled, which was pretty much the whole idea behind this little getaway.
